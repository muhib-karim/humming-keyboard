Everything a keyboard should do. Voice that stays yours.

One app on each platform, the same speech models and the same clean-up rules everywhere.

Words land as you speak

Tap the mic and talk. Your sentence appears in the text field as an underlined draft that corrects itself in place, then is typed once when you stop. It keeps listening with the screen off or in another app.

  • Clean mode drops um and uh (there is a switch to keep them) and applies self-corrections: "3, no, 4pm" becomes "4pm".
  • Spoken commands: "new line", "comma", "scratch that", "bullet point", and in 1.7 "colon", quotes, brackets and "delete last word".
  • Custom words, so names come out spelled your way, and replacements: say "my sig", get your signature.

A full keyboard, not just a mic

Built on FlorisBoard and tuned for everyday typing, with prediction that reads the whole sentence and runs on the phone.

  • Word prediction and autocorrect in six languages, with an on-device neural next-word model for English.
  • Glide typing, emoji search, stickers, and optional GIF search.
  • Clipboard history: 100 items by default, nothing deleted automatically unless you ask.
  • Light, dark and AMOLED themes, your own photo, one-handed, floating and split layouts.

Your computer, too

Hold Ctrl + Shift + Space (Cmd on a Mac) in any app, talk, let go: the text is pasted where your cursor is. The same Parakeet models as Android, downloaded once and checked against a pinned SHA-256.

  • Windows, macOS and Linux, from a tray or menu-bar app.
  • Searchable history on the computer, a personal dictionary, spoken lists as bullets.
  • Signed self-updates: the app refuses any file not signed with Humming's update key.

And on iPhone

The app records with Apple's speech recogniser with on-device recognition required: it refuses to run rather than fall back to a server. iOS keyboards can't use the microphone, so you dictate in the app and the Humming keyboard inserts the result in one tap.

  • The same Clean mode, Accuracy mode and custom words as Android and desktop.
  • Distributed as an unsigned .ipa you sideload with your own Apple ID.

New in 1.7

Gaps closed after a teardown of Gboard, SwiftKey, the Bangla keyboards and the dictation apps.

  • Bangla phonetic typing. Add Bengali with a QWERTY layout and type the way Avro users do: ami bangla likhi becomes আমি বাংলা লিখি, one word at a time, with the Latin word one tap away.
  • Dictation that fits the sentence. Dictate into the middle of a line and Humming lowercases the first word, skips the stray space after a bracket and drops the final period when your sentence carries on.
  • Dictation like the dictation apps. Say "bullet point" for a list item, "scratch that" to drop the last sentence, and write "spoken -> written" in custom words to turn a phrase into anything. Filler removal now has its own switch. On Android, desktop and iPhone.
  • More spoken punctuation. Colon, semicolon, quotes, brackets, ellipsis and "delete last word", the same on Android, desktop and iPhone.

These shipped in 1.7.0 and are covered by unit tests that run in CI. 1.7.1 adds no features: it adds the emulator test, more unit tests and the quality gates shown under Measured.

Private by design, and checked in code

No account, no analytics, no ads. The offline voice path has no network code, and a unit test fails the build if any file outside a short list uses a network API.

Stays on your device

  • Your voice. In the default Offline mode, audio is recorded and transcribed on the device.
  • What you type. Suggestions and corrections are computed on the phone; learned words never leave it.
  • Clipboard and history. Kept on the device, and skipped entirely in incognito.

The only times it goes online

  1. The one-time speech model download, checked against a pinned SHA-256.
  2. The update check of the sideloaded app (on by default, sends no personal data; switch it off in Settings).
  3. GIF search, off by default: only the words you type in its search box.
  4. AI clean-up with your own key, off by default: the finished text, never audio.
  5. Online mode, off by default: hands audio to the phone's own speech service.

Questions

Does voice typing really work without the internet?

Yes, in the default Offline mode. The first time you tap the mic on Android or open the desktop app, Humming downloads the speech model once (104 MB for the Fast model). After that, dictation needs no connection. On iPhone, Humming requires Apple's on-device recognition and refuses to run rather than use a server.

Which languages can I dictate in?

Offline voice typing is English today, on every platform. On Android, the optional Online mode uses your phone's own speech service and its languages, but then audio leaves the phone. Typing works in many layouts, with prediction in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese, and Bangla phonetic typing from 1.7.

How do I type Bangla phonetically?

In Settings, Languages & layouts, add Bengali and choose the QWERTY layout. Type romanised Bangla; each word is suggested in Bangla script and space or punctuation types it. Capitals follow the Avro convention (T, D, N, R, Sh for the retroflex letters; I, U, O for long vowels). The fixed Bengali layout is still there if you prefer it.

Why is the iPhone app a sideload?

It is not on the App Store yet, so releases ship as an unsigned .ipa. You sign it with your own free Apple ID through Sideloadly, AltStore or Xcode and re-sign it every 7 days. The sideload guide walks through it.

Why do Windows and macOS warn me on first launch?

The desktop builds are not yet signed with a Microsoft or Apple developer certificate, so the system shows a one-time warning. Updates after that are signed with Humming's own update key and checked before they install.

What does the update check send?

It fetches a small public file to see whether a newer version exists. It sends no audio, no typed text and no identifiers; the hosting providers see your IP address, as with any download. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings, Updates. The Google Play version leaves updates to Play.

Is it free? What's the catch?

Free, with no ads, no account and no paid tier. The code is licensed Apache-2.0. The optional AI clean-up uses your own API key with the provider you choose, and is off by default.
