Tap the mic and talk. The words are typed as you speak, for as long as you speak, even with the screen off. Speech recognition runs on your device, so dictation works in airplane mode and your voice never leaves it.

Why Humming

Offline on every platform

NVIDIA Parakeet on Android and desktop, Apple's on-device recogniser on iPhone. No server fallback.

No caps, no time limit

Dictate for an hour like it's ten seconds. No weekly word allowance, no account.

Clean text

Clean mode drops um and uh and applies "3, no, 4pm" → "4pm". Accuracy mode also removes hedge fillers.

A real keyboard

Prediction, glide typing, emoji, clipboard, themes and a floating keyboard on Android; a Humming keyboard on iPhone.

Custom words

Names and terms spelled your way every time, on every platform.

Free and open source

Apache-2.0. Built and tested in public CI, down to the word error rate.
