Humming prediction data

lexicon_en.tsv - 155,857 English words, each with a smoothed log probability (log P = q / 10 - 24) and flags
(o = possibly offensive, never suggested unless allowed; a = abbreviation).
  * Words and frequency classes: en_US_wordlist.combined from the Android Open Source Project LatinIME
    dictionaries (packages/inputmethods/LatinIME/dictionaries). Copyright (C) The Android Open Source Project.
    Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  * Frequencies blended 50/50 with word counts from the Tatoeba English sentences (see below).
  * A few chat words added by Humming (lol, omg, idk, tbh, ...); most all-caps abbreviations removed.

lexicon_de.tsv, lexicon_es.tsv, lexicon_fr.tsv, lexicon_it.tsv, lexicon_pt.tsv - the 60,000 most frequent words of
the German, Spanish, French, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese wordlists (de, es, fr, it, pt_BR _wordlist.combined)
from the same Android Open Source Project LatinIME dictionaries, with their frequency classes (log P = f / 10 - 24)
and the "possibly offensive" marks. Copyright (C) The Android Open Source Project. Apache License, Version 2.0.

ngrams_en.tsv - next-word statistics (one- and two-word contexts, top following words with counts), counted from
the English sentences of the Tatoeba Project (https://tatoeba.org), downloaded 2026-09-30.
  Tatoeba sentences are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/ . Only aggregated counts are distributed, no sentences.
  Common placeholder names used in the corpus are removed from the predictions.
  Tatoeba is a translation corpus, so about twenty everyday messaging contexts ("see you", "on my", "let me",
  "running", "no", ...) are boosted towards chat phrasing by a short hand-written list (Humming, Apache-2.0).

ngrams_de.tsv, ngrams_es.tsv, ngrams_fr.tsv, ngrams_it.tsv, ngrams_pt.tsv - next-word statistics (one- and two-word
contexts, top following words with counts) counted by Humming's keyboard/lm/ngrams.py from:
  * the German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese sentences of the Tatoeba Project (https://tatoeba.org),
    downloaded 2026-09-30, Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR),
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/ ;
  * the sentence lists of Mozilla Common Voice (github.com/common-voice/common-voice, server/data/<language>:
    sentence-collector.txt, and frasi.txt for Italian), dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0,
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
  Only aggregated counts are distributed, no sentences. 1 % of sentences (by hash) are held out for testing.

hlm_en.bin - Humming's English neural next-word model: a word-level LSTM (16,000 words, 128-dimensional tied
embeddings, 256 hidden units, int8 weights) trained by keyboard/lm/train.py on the English sentences of Tatoeba
(CC BY 2.0 FR, see above) and Common Voice (sentence-collector.txt, CC0 1.0), minus the held-out 1 %. The model
weights are Humming's own work, Apache License 2.0; the vocabulary is drawn from lexicon_en.tsv.

emoji_en.tsv - English word -> emoji hints for the suggestion strip, derived from the Unicode CLDR v45 emoji
annotations already bundled by FlorisBoard (assets/ime/media/emoji/en.txt; Unicode License v3,
https://www.unicode.org/license.txt).
