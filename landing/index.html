Humming

Talk, and it types.

Hold a key, speak, let go. Humming turns your voice into clean, punctuated text right where your cursor is, in any app. It runs entirely on your device: no account, no cloud, no recordings kept.

You say: "um so lets meet at 3, no wait, 4 pm. first, eggs, second, milk"
Humming types: "So let's meet at 4pm. First, eggs, second, milk." (or a bulleted list, your choice)

Private by design

Speech recognition runs on your phone or computer with NVIDIA Parakeet. Nothing is uploaded.

Clean, not literal

Fillers, false starts and "no wait" corrections disappear; punctuation and capitals appear.

Works everywhere

A full keyboard on Android; a shortcut that types into any app on Windows and macOS.

Your words

A personal dictionary for names and jargon, and a searchable history that stays on your device.

How to open it

The Windows and macOS builds are unsigned: they are not signed with an Apple or Microsoft developer certificate yet, so the first launch shows a warning. The app itself is the same either way.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Humming to Applications.
  2. Open Humming. macOS blocks it the first time ("cannot be opened" / "could not verify"). Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Humming.
  4. Confirm with Open (and your password). After that it opens normally.

Apple's guide: Open a Mac app from an unknown developer.

Windows

  1. Run the installer (.exe or .msi).
  2. SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC" (unknown publisher). Click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway, then finish the installer.
